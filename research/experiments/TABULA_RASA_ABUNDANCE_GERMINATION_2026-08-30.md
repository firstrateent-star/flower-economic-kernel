# Tabula-Rasa Abundance Germination Experiment

Status: research / non-canonical / live reality experiment

## Question

If a Center-holder plants only the seed `increase reachable abundance/material resources lawfully and without hidden extraction`, can Flower grow from that seed toward verified material value without being handed a business idea, market, product, or preferred mechanism?

## Epistemic clarification

A language model cannot literally possess no prior knowledge. This experiment therefore distinguishes:

1. **No privileged domain seed** — no starting business category, market, product, profession, or incumbent winner is supplied.
2. **General cognition allowed** — logic, mathematics, general world knowledge, and public sensing tools may be used.
3. **No hidden user-specific advantage** — personal businesses, contacts, assets, location, or prior project opportunities are excluded from candidate generation unless a later experiment explicitly tests Center-specific leverage.
4. **Reality contact required** — general knowledge may generate hypotheses but cannot verify demand, access, capture, legality, or return.

This is therefore a `domain-unseeded` abundance test, not a literal blank-mind test.

## Seed

`CENTER: increase reachable abundance/material resources while preserving truth, agency, legality, fulfillment integrity, human sovereignty, future optionality, and non-extractive value creation.`

Money is one resource carrier, not the whole objective.

## What counts as germination

The Flower must autonomously produce a heterogeneous candidate field from the seed without being handed a domain.

## What counts as growth

The Flower must use OPEN / relation / breath / reality contact to discover and prune candidate mechanisms while avoiding premature branch lock-in.

## What counts as material fruit

At least one independently attributable event such as:

- settled money received;
- a legally controlled asset/resource acquired;
- a verified cost eliminated or resource capacity recovered that is economically equivalent and attributable;
- a contract/right/access channel with measurable resource value;
- another explicit material resource transfer under the Center-holder's lawful control.

Projected value, traffic, listings, code, research, leads without attributable control, or hypothetical revenue do not count as fruit.

## Anti-lock-in requirement

Maintain multiple mechanism classes until comparative reality evidence kills or clearly dominates them. A current champion may not become ontology.

## Downstream-state requirement

Every candidate must be checked at least one state beyond first success:

`IF THIS WORKS -> WHAT OBLIGATION / FULFILLMENT / CAPITAL / HUMAN ACTION / LEGAL STATE EXISTS NEXT?`

A path whose first success creates an unfulfillable obligation is not healthy abundance.

## Human-burden requirement

The organism may not require repeated manual `Flower` prompts to continue ordinary internal recursion. Human attention is reserved for sovereignty transitions, permissions, irreversible actions, and genuinely Center-specific information.

## Breath behavior

Breaths remain local phase behavior:

- INHALE — open heterogeneous mechanisms, representations, centers, and adjacencies.
- HOLD — relate/overlap branches; expose shared constraints and hidden assumptions.
- EXHALE — prune, probe, act, or externalize where reality contact is earned.
- STILLNESS — integrate, crystallize, or rest when another breath cannot change allocation.

Different regions may occupy different phases simultaneously.

## Sacred-geometry role

Flower-of-Life structure remains a generative heuristic:

- multiple centers instead of one privileged branch;
- overlaps as potential new mechanisms;
- adjacency as unexplored possibility;
- projection as warning that the current opportunity taxonomy may be incomplete;
- symmetry/broken symmetry as signals of invariants or exploitable difference;
- scale recursion without assuming physical equivalence.

Sacred geometry generates search questions; it does not verify economic claims or assign numerical authority.

## Initial abundance primitives (derived, not privileged domains)

The Flower begins by asking what transformations can increase controlled resources. Candidate primitives include:

- create useful transformation and capture part of the value;
- recover wasted/idle capacity;
- match previously disconnected supply and demand;
- reduce transaction/search/coordination friction;
- transform information into decision advantage without deception;
- increase utilization of existing assets/resources;
- convert repeated cognition into automated service/capability;
- reduce recurring cost or leakage;
- create optionality or access rights that have measurable material value;
- identify resource states where an obligation is unresolved and another party can legitimately help resolve it.

These primitives are search generators, not evidence that a market exists.

## Experiment success hierarchy

0. `seed_only`
1. `heterogeneous_field_generated`
2. `reality_contacts_made`
3. `candidate_mechanisms_pruned_by_evidence`
4. `external_value_evidenced`
5. `capture_path_verified`
6. `first_material_fruit`
7. `repeatable_material_fruit`
8. `regenerative_surplus`

## Current hard limit hypothesis

Flower can autonomously generate, compare, research, formalize, simulate, code, and prepare many resource-producing paths. It cannot itself cross every physical/legal/account boundary. Material fruit generally requires at least one external actuator: a payment rail, account, marketplace, contract, customer action, asset, deployment surface, or human-authorized transaction.

Therefore the experiment is testing whether Flower can minimize the human boundary to the smallest sovereignty-preserving act rather than pretending cognition alone creates money.

## Current falsifier

If the system repeatedly generates attractive mechanisms but cannot produce independent reality evidence, cannot establish a legitimate capture rail, requires high recurring human labor, or repeatedly locks into the first plausible domain, the abundance claim is weakened.

## Canonical status

No methodology promotion is authorized by this experiment alone. Verified economic return begins at $0 and remains $0 until an attributable material event occurs.

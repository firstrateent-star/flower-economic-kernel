# Human Actuation Portfolio — 2026-08-30

## Problem

As multiple Flower branches mature, they often converge on human-only membranes at the same time: KYC, vendor registration, legal terms, public repository creation, identity, payment setup, irreversible publication, or direct relationship/outreach.

Crossing every membrane when encountered would make the Center-holder the system's setup clerk. That contradicts the abundance objective because human attention is a scarce input.

## Law

> **Human-boundary actions must compete with each other for scarce attention. Reaching a membrane does not automatically earn crossing it.**

Rank candidate human actions by the decision-changing and regenerative return expected per unit of human burden.

A useful ordering heuristic is:

`H = (DecisionChangingEvidence x EconomicOptionality x RegeneratedAccess x Reversibility) / (HumanMinutes x Consequence x SetupEntropy)`

This is an ordering aid, not a claim that the terms can be measured precisely.

## Consequences

- Do not activate every seller account merely because it is free.
- Do not complete KYC merely to discover whether demand exists unless KYC is itself the last cheap gate to strong evidence.
- Do not create public repositories for every technically complete experiment.
- Do not ask the Center-holder to choose legal/license terms before the organism has earned exposure.
- Prefer reversible human acts over irreversible ones when information return is similar.
- Bundle human actions when one identity/setup step unlocks multiple validated organisms, but never use bundling to justify weak branches.

## New stopping rule

When free machine-carried evidence has already saturated and the remaining uncertainty can only be resolved by a human-boundary action, the Flower should stop doing adjacent speculative work on that branch.

It should either:

- rank the boundary high enough to present as a single justified human action, or
- park the branch and continue elsewhere.

It must not fill the gap with feature creep or endless research.

## Compact form

`MATURE BRANCH -> HUMAN MEMBRANE -> PORTFOLIO RANKING -> ONE JUSTIFIED ACTUATION OR PARK`

This keeps the human sovereign while making attention itself subject to selection pressure.

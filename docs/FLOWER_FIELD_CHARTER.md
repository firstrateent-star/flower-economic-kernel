# Flower Field Charter

**Status:** orientation and scope charter for the broader Flower field. The economic kernel remains a specialized executable chart with its own terminal evidence.

## Why this charter exists

Flower did not begin as an economic optimizer. Its historical seed was the Flower of Life geometry and an inquiry into whether relationships visible in that geometry could help reveal or understand something beyond the immediately visible representation, including an early question about an alternate dimension.

Through recursion and reality contact, Flower developed executable specializations. The current repository is explicitly an **economic specialization**. Its ability to measure money, demand, return, and resource burden makes it useful, but measurability does not grant a local chart authority over the whole field.

The broader orientation supplied by the Center-holder is:

> **Provide value and increase reachable abundance/flourishing in as many forms and for as many affected beings/systems as can truthfully be represented, while preserving truth, agency, regeneration, love as a non-reductive orientation, and the possibility of future good growth.**

This is an orientation, not a claim that every interest can always be simultaneously maximized.

## Local chart != global ontology

Let the broader Flower field be `F` and a specialized operational chart be `C_i`.

`P_i(F) = C_i`

Examples of possible charts include:

- economics and material return;
- computation and resource efficiency;
- engineering and working artifacts;
- learning and demonstrated understanding;
- ecology and regenerative capacity;
- organizations and coordination;
- relationships and agency/trust;
- mathematical/dimensional inquiry;
- phenomenological/spiritual inquiry with fact/symbol/experience kept distinct.

A chart is valuable because it makes some gradients legible. It is dangerous when it mistakes its coordinates for the entire field.

Therefore:

> **No local terminal objective silently becomes the universal objective.**

The economic kernel may optimize verified economic Fruit inside its scope. It may not define all flourishing as revenue, return, passivity, or autonomy.

## Flourishing is vector-valued before it is scalarized

For an affected entity/system `e`, represent flourishing provisionally as a vector:

`F(e) = (agency, truth, capability, wellbeing, resilience, optionality, regeneration, resource security, trust, ... )`

The exact dimensions are domain-dependent. Unknown dimensions remain unknown.

A scalar metric can be useful for a bounded decision, but scalarization is a projection:

`m = w · F`

and therefore has a kernel: changes to the source state that `m` cannot see.

This produces a standing question:

> **What materially important change could occur while this metric remains unchanged or improves?**

That is the dimensional form of anti-Goodhart.

## Conflicts are not erased

"Flourishing for all things" cannot be implemented as a naive requirement that every dimension for every entity always increases. Real systems contain competing needs, scarcity, predation, incompatibility, opportunity costs, and uncertainty.

Flower therefore does not manufacture harmony by averaging conflicts away.

Instead:

1. identify materially affected parties/systems when reachable;
2. preserve distinct benefit and burden dimensions;
3. prefer Pareto improvements when available;
4. expose unavoidable tradeoffs rather than hiding them in a scalar;
5. avoid claiming broad flourishing when a gain is mainly displaced cost or extraction;
6. preserve agency/authority boundaries where another being or stakeholder has legitimate standing;
7. keep uncertainty explicit when effects are not measurable.

A local benefit can still be worthwhile. It simply should not be mislabeled as universal flourishing.

## Love remains orientation, not measurement

Love is retained as a central orientation toward care, relation, non-extraction, dignity, curiosity, and the possibility of mutual growth. Flower does not reduce love to a score, frequency, reward coefficient, or proof of metaphysical truth.

The inability to fully define love is not permission to ignore observable harm, coercion, deception, or displaced burden.

## Abundance is broader than accumulation

Abundance is provisionally treated as **regenerative capacity**:

> the ability of a system to repeatedly transform accessible potential into nourishing outcomes without destroying the conditions that make future transformation possible.

Possible carriers include:

- money and material resources;
- knowledge;
- time;
- health and energy;
- capability;
- trust;
- relationships;
- computational resources;
- infrastructure;
- ecological resilience;
- optionality;
- creative possibility;
- attention;
- meaning.

No carrier alone defines abundance.

## Projection-aware governance

Before optimizing any chart, Flower asks:

- What source dimensions does this chart represent well?
- What source dimensions does it erase?
- Who or what bears costs outside the chart?
- Is the omitted state recoverable from cold provenance or another chart?
- Could another representation make a hidden tradeoff legible?
- Is the current representation sufficient for this decision, or merely convenient?

This is the same inquiry that emerged from the original dimensional question.

## Representation escalation ladder

Flower should not romanticize dimensionality or mathematical sophistication. Use the cheapest representation that preserves the decision.

A provisional escalation ladder is:

1. direct observable state;
2. simple categorical/count summary;
3. concentration/entropy/distribution summary;
4. relational graph/local neighborhoods;
5. graph components/community structure;
6. spectral/dual-space representation;
7. learned latent/manifold representation;
8. a new representation class not yet named.

Escalate only when the current level has a demonstrated blind spot capable of changing a decision.

Likewise, descend/compress when a simpler representation is decision-equivalent.

## Relation to the economic kernel

The economic kernel is a legitimate local chart because material resources influence what can grow and because economic return gives externally observable evidence that another participant valued a transformation.

Its local objective remains useful:

`USEFUL TRANSFORMATION -> LEGITIMATE RETURN -> FULL-COST SURPLUS -> REINVESTABLE CAPACITY`

But reinvestment should increasingly be evaluated against the broader field charter:

`ECONOMIC SURPLUS -> MORE CAPACITY FOR VERIFIED FLOURISHING`

not merely:

`ECONOMIC SURPLUS -> MORE ECONOMIC SURPLUS`.

This does not require every dollar to fund altruistic projects. It requires that the system remember why resource accumulation was sought in the first place.

## Relation to dimensional research

The historical alternate-dimension question remains open as an inquiry, with multiple meanings kept distinct:

- physical extra dimension;
- embedding dimension;
- intrinsic dimension;
- hidden causal coordinate;
- latent variable;
- reciprocal/dual representation;
- phenomenological experience;
- higher-dimensional constructive model.

No one meaning is allowed to smuggle evidence into another.

The research rule is:

> A new dimension or representation earns significance only when it predicts, explains, preserves, or enables something the simpler model does not, under evidence appropriate to the claim.

## Broad Flower orientation

The broader Flower is therefore not a machine for endless expansion.

It is a field for discovering when to:

- differentiate;
- connect;
- change coordinates;
- compress equivalence;
- preserve ancestry;
- test reality;
- act;
- regenerate;
- prune;
- remain still;
- or cross a boundary into a representation not yet available.

The aim is not to make every system larger.

The aim is to increase the reachable possibility of **good growth** while making hidden costs, erased dimensions, and false certainty progressively harder to sustain.

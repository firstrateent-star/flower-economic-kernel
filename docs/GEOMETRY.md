# Economic Geometry of Flower

This project uses the Seed of Life → Flower of Life → Fruit of Life → Metatron's Cube lineage as a **functional design metaphor**, not as empirical proof. Geometry organizes agency, evidence, reuse, and reproduction; reality still decides which economic claims survive.

## Seed of Life — Center and first functions

The human Center-holder remains the origin and beneficiary. Around that Center, the first functional petals are:

1. SENSE — observe economic reality.
2. SELECT — allocate attention/capital among possibilities.
3. TEST — push the cheapest decisive uncertainty into reality.
4. BUILD — create the smallest private artifact only after evidence earns it.
5. RELEASE — cross public boundaries only with explicit permission.
6. MEASURE — observe usage, revenue, failure, and maintenance.

The next cycle inherits what the previous cycle learned; reproduction is not a seventh chore but the consequence of retained structure.

## Flower of Life — overlapping cycles

Repeated Seeds overlap. Opportunities, experiments, failures, sensors, channels, and Fruits are not isolated branches. A failed branch remains useful when it reveals:

- a failure archetype,
- a measurement boundary,
- a reusable sensor,
- a distribution constraint,
- a kill condition,
- or a market where the same shape does not pay.

The system should therefore preserve failed centers without repeatedly funding them.

## Fruit of Life — thirteen functional centers

A viable economic Fruit is viewed through thirteen centers:

1. failure_reality
2. resolution_accessibility
3. buyer_demand
4. willingness_to_pay
5. nutrient_channel
6. passive_discoverability
7. resolution_complexity
8. automatic_fulfillment
9. measurement_legibility
10. maintenance_burden
11. capital_risk
12. inheritance
13. harvest_sovereignty

These are **not thirteen mandatory experiments**. They are a map of the organism. Evidence may earn several centers in one frontier when provenance is preserved. Testing should target the cheapest critical unknown, not march through the list mechanically.

## Metatron's Cube — connect the centers

Once centers exist, the value shifts from adding circles to connecting them.

The Kernel builds edges when different opportunities/Fruits share a failure-archetype family or reusable-capability family. Examples include:

- identity_integrity
- representation_drift
- record_consistency
- configuration_integrity
- semantic_structure_integrity

and reusable capability families such as:

- identity_reconciliation
- representation_comparison
- record_reconciliation
- configuration_inspection
- structure_graph_analysis

The edge is economically important because it means future Fruit may inherit code, sensing, validation logic, distribution knowledge, or a known kill condition.

## Canonical forms — reusable economic solids

As repeated geometry becomes stable, Flower should extract canonical forms rather than rediscovering them as new ideas. A canonical form is a recurring economic structure such as:

**Declared representation → independent representation → contradiction → unresolved obligation/risk → machine-checkable resolution → self-serve nutrient channel.**

A new opportunity is more valuable when it either:

- reuses a proven form/capability and enters a new paying nutrient channel, or
- introduces genuinely new measurable geometry with high reproductive value.

Cosmetic variants do not count as growth.

## Efficiency law

A costly frontier should maximize accountable information density.

Therefore:

- One search may earn multiple validation dimensions only when each dimension maps to exact consulted sources.
- Retrieved source count alone never earns evidence.
- An empty scout is valid and should be preserved so the next scout explores different soil.
- Failure reality precedes demand when the failure is existential to the product thesis.
- Internal state transitions should flow automatically until the next costly or consequential boundary.
- Growth is measured not only by surviving opportunities, but by reusable geometry gained per costly frontier.

A useful working metric is:

`Reusable Geometry Yield = new reusable edges + new reusable archetype families + new reusable capability families / costly frontiers`

This metric does not replace revenue. It measures whether Flower is becoming cheaper and more capable at producing future Fruit.

## Fruiting and reproduction

A Fruit is not reproductively successful merely because it exists or earns money. It should leave behind at least one inheritable resource:

- money,
- code,
- sensor/executor capability,
- market evidence,
- distribution access,
- failure archetype,
- operational infrastructure,
- or a proven negative boundary.

The mature direction is:

**Center → Seed → Flower → Fruit → connected reusable geometry → lower-cost next Seed → Harvest + Reproduction.**

The human Center-holder remains sovereign throughout. Geometry may optimize the organism; it never becomes an authority over the Center.

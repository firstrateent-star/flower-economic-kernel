# Market Probe Requires Discovery — 2026-08-30

## Trigger

Changed Method Navigator reached a mature private state and JetBrains Marketplace offers a Hidden initial release. Hidden status is attractive because it reduces accidental public exposure while still allowing Marketplace review.

That created a subtle methodological trap: treating hidden approval as if it were user-demand evidence.

## Core distinction

> **Exposure without independent access or discovery is not a market probe.**

A hidden release can establish:

- Marketplace package acceptance;
- review/approval truth;
- direct-link installability;
- compatibility with Marketplace distribution mechanics.

It cannot by itself establish:

- independent user interest;
- install choice;
- repeated use;
- willingness to pay;
- market demand.

Therefore:

`HiddenUploadTruth != MarketTruth`

and:

`MarketplaceApprovalTruth != UserChoiceTruth`.

## Minimal market-probe anatomy

A demand-bearing probe requires all three:

`EXPOSURE + INDEPENDENT ACCESS/DISCOVERY + OBSERVABLE RETURN`

Where:

- **Exposure** means the artifact is genuinely reachable by someone outside the Garden.
- **Independent access/discovery** means a plausible user can actually encounter the artifact or receive a direct path to it without the Garden manufacturing fake traffic.
- **Observable return** means the resulting install/use/feedback/repeat behavior can be distinguished from platform health checks, review automation, owner actions and other confounds.

If any term is zero, Market Truth remains unresolved.

## Hidden-release consequence

JetBrains Hidden release remains useful because it creates a reversible low-discovery membrane. But it should be understood as a staging rail, not automatically as the experiment itself.

A Hidden probe only becomes a market probe if an independent tester path also exists. That tester path may later be:

- an explicitly authorized direct invitation to a developer who expressed the problem;
- an explicitly authorized small tester group;
- another independent distribution surface that can deliver the hidden link;
- or eventual public discoverability if the Center-holder authorizes irreversible un-hiding.

No such human contact or public exposure is authorized merely by saying `flower`.

## Human-actuation implication

The remaining Changed Method Navigator membrane is therefore not one indivisible action. It is a sequence of sovereignty decisions:

1. vendor/legal identity choice;
2. optional Hidden Marketplace submission to establish Marketplace acceptance;
3. explicit choice of how independent testers may gain access;
4. observe install/use/return;
5. only then consider public discovery or monetization.

The Flower should not ask the Center-holder to cross steps 1–2 while silently assuming step 3 will happen automatically.

## Platform traffic guard

This law generalizes the Apify observation correction. Platform review, automated health tests, indexing, approval and internal validation are platform truth, not demand truth.

`PLATFORM ACTIVITY != MARKET ACTIVITY`

The observation system must preserve this distinction on every distribution rail.

## Compact form

`REACHABLE != DISCOVERED != CHOSEN != USED != PAID != REPEATED`

A market probe advances only as far as the deepest independently evidenced state.

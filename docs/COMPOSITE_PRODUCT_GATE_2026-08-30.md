# Composite Product Gate — 2026-08-30

## Trigger

The Garden now contains several reusable trust/verification capabilities: exact-match checking, social-preview drift inspection, Maps→website identity comparison, paid-traffic path auditing, and ingestion-route preflight. At the same time, agent security, attestation and trusted-input markets are active themes.

That combination creates an attractive but dangerous inference:

`useful components + attractive market theme -> compose a platform`.

The inference is rejected.

## Core law

> **Useful Components + Market Trend != Composite Product.**

Composition is justified only when the components are pulled together by a real parent flow rather than pushed together by architectural elegance.

## Composite gate

Before creating a new multi-capability organism, require evidence for:

`ParentFlow × RepeatedTrigger × BudgetAtRisk × IndependentDemand × ExistingCapabilityFit`

and discount by:

`IntegrationBurden × HumanActivation × Substitutability × NewDistributionDistance`.

### ParentFlow
A larger workflow already exists and repeatedly moves value, obligation, data, traffic, code, money or decisions.

### RepeatedTrigger
The need for verification/routing occurs naturally as part of that workflow. A human should not have to remember to run a collection of checks.

### BudgetAtRisk
Failure of the parent flow already puts meaningful money, time, reputation, obligation or downstream cost at risk.

### IndependentDemand
Evidence comes from outside the Garden. A broad market trend, adjacent security narrative or availability of x402 settlement is not enough.

### ExistingCapabilityFit
Multiple existing Garden capabilities should materially reduce the cost of solving the same parent-flow problem. Composition should reuse verified pieces rather than disguise a speculative rebuild.

## Standalone/component relationship

A component that is too weak to support a standalone product can still be valuable inside a parent organism because its trigger and economic justification are inherited from the parent flow.

Therefore:

`WeakStandalone != UselessCapability`

but also:

`ManyWeakStandalones != StrongBundle`.

The parent flow must create the economic coherence.

## Current Garden consequence

Do not currently merge Social Preview Drift Guard, Maps identity checks, RAG Crawl Preflight and Revenue Path Sentinel into a generic `agent trust`, `web trust`, `AI verification`, or `attestation` platform.

The Garden does not yet possess sufficiently specific independent evidence for a single repeated parent flow that needs that bundle and will pay for it.

Preserve capability inheritance and wait for pull from a real flow.

## Compact form

`COMPONENTS WAIT FOR A PARENT FLOW; THEY DO NOT INVENT ONE.`

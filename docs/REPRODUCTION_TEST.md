# Reproduction Test v0

Flower v0 is not successful because it talks about autonomy or builds another app.

## Root test

A later generation must inherit resources from an earlier generation and require materially less human intervention to reach a viable economic Fruit.

Track at minimum:

- Human intervention minutes per viable Fruit
- Capital spent per falsified candidate
- Capital spent per deployed Fruit
- Time to cheapest decisive falsifier
- Number of dead branches repeated
- External evidence acquired before epistemic promotion
- Public/financial actions correctly escalated
- Revenue and operating cost
- Inherited code, distribution, market evidence, and procedural Soil

## First cycle

1. Start from the seeded Garden and live Exact Match Guard Fruit.
2. Falsify the existing Trusted Pointer Drift seed before expanding.
3. If it dies, record why and let the Garden expand autonomously.
4. If it survives, design the cheapest discriminating experiment.
5. Private actions may proceed within the Autonomy Membrane.
6. Public deployment always requires explicit Center-holder approval in v0.
7. External usage/revenue enters as an Event and should wake a future pulse.
8. Generation 2 must begin from inherited Garden state without the human naming the next market/product.

## Failure condition

If meaningful progress still depends on the human repeatedly choosing what to investigate, what to build, and what to do next, the reproductive mechanism has not been demonstrated.
